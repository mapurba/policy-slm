# 4.0 Lifecycle Business Process for the Workday Driver

Workday HCM (Human Capital Management) system has multiple lifecycle business processes available for hiring, termination, move worker position, and attribute management for an employee or contingent worker. The lifecycle business process feature is available with Workday 1.3 and above.
