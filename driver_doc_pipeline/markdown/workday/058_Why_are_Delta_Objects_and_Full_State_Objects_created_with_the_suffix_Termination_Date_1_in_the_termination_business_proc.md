# 10.2 Why are Delta Objects and Full State Objects created with the suffix “<Termination Date + 1>” in the termination business process?

This occurs because, the termination is in effect from the end of termination date. Hence, the termination information, such as Active status, Termination Date, Termination Reason, Regrettable or non-Regrettable, Voluntary or non-Voluntary are suffixed.
